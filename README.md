# EatNVibe Analytics

Q3 2026 sales analysis for the marketing team.

- data/q3_2026_sales.csv: units sold per month and product (July to September 2026). Single bars are counted in bars. The Variety Pack is counted in packs (12 bars each).
- analysis/q3_sales_analysis.py: computes revenue per product and total Q3 revenue.

Prices: single bar $3.49, Variety Pack $36.00.
Strawberry Fine is not included (launching January 2027).
